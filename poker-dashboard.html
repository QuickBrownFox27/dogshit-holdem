The Bookie

Prices to win the next game for ranked players (3+ games). Built from win rate, average finishing position, overall profit and form over the last 4 games, with a 12% bookie's margin. Bragging rights only.

How the odds work
  1. The field. Anyone with 3+ games in total who played at least one of the last 4 games.
  2. Win rate. Wins ÷ games, padded with two imaginary average nights (about a 1-in-4.5 chance each) so a short record can't go extreme. One win from one game doesn't make you a certainty.
  3. Finishing. Where you usually finish, allowing for how many played. Always first scores 1, always last 0, average 0.5. Padded the same way.
  4. Profit. Lifetime profit ÷ total buy-ins (plus $100 of padding), turned into a factor between 0.5 and 1.5.
  5. Form. Your last 4 games: a win counts 1.5, getting your buy-in back 1.15, anything else 0.8. Averaged.
  6. Score = win rate × √(finishing factor × profit factor) × form. The square root keeps finishing and profit as a nudge, not a takeover.
  7. Prices. Scores become chances, the bookie adds a 12% margin (like a real bookmaker), and each is rounded to the nearest standard price.

Weights are judgement calls, not fitted to the results, and a handful of games is a small sample. Treat them as bragging rights.
