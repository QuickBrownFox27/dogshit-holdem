How the odds work
  1. The field. Starts as every ranked player (3+ games) who played at least one of the last 4 games. Tick or untick names under "Who's in?" to price the actual table.
  2. Recent games count more. Everything below is time-weighted with a 6-game half-life: last night counts in full, a game 6 comp games ago counts half, 12 games ago a quarter. Missed games still age your old results.
  3. Wins against expected. Each game is worth 1 ÷ the number of players that night, so a win at a 6-player table counts for more than one at a 3-player table. Your wins are compared with what an average player would expect from the same nights, padded with two imaginary average games so a short record stays close to average.
  4. Finishing. Where you usually finish, allowing for table size. Always first scores 1, always last 0, average 0.5. Padded the same way.
  5. Profit. Profit ÷ buy-ins (plus $100 of padding), turned into a factor between 0.5 and 1.5.
  6. Score = wins factor × √(finishing factor × profit factor). The square root keeps finishing and profit as adjustments, not a takeover. There's no separate form factor: the time weighting already does that job, without counting a recent win twice.
  7. Prices. Scores become chances, the bookie adds a 12% margin, and each is rounded to the nearest standard price. Rounding shifts the margin a little, so the real total is shown under the prices as "Book".

The Bookie always uses full history and ignores the filters. Weights are judgement calls, not fitted to the results, and a handful of games is a small sample. The prices are for entertainment.

What the numbers mean

Win
Finished first, including chops. A shared first place counts as a win for everyone who shared it (marked *).
Payout
Took any money home at all, even less than the buy-in.
Recovered buy-in
Net result of $0 or better: got at least the buy-in back.
Profitable night
Net result strictly above $0.
Net result
Payout minus buy-ins for the night. Buy-ins include recorded rebuys.
ROI
Net profit ÷ total buy-ins, as a percentage.
Best night
The highest net profit in one game. The biggest payout (gross money taken home) is tracked separately.
Max drawdown
The biggest fall from a running cumulative-profit peak, starting from zero at the first game in view.
Rolling 3
Average net result over up to the last three matching appearances. The first two points use one and two games.
Est. rebuys
Estimated from each player's buy-in total against the smallest buy-in that night. The spreadsheet records totals, not individual rebuys.
Ranked
Three career games are needed for a ledger place, counted up to the latest game in the current view. Unranked players are still shown and can be analysed.
Sample size
Most numbers rest on a handful of nights. Patterns are for fun and discussion, not proof of skill.

The dashboard doesn't record hands, hours or blinds, so there are no hourly rates or hand-level stats.
